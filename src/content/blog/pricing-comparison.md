# A transparent price comparison of change management tools

Published pricing is the exception rather than the norm across change management
software vendors. Most list pages default to "contact us for a quote," which means
evaluating options typically requires a sales conversation before budget fit can even be
confirmed. This piece compiles what is publicly documented, sourced directly from vendor
pricing pages and third-party contract-benchmark data, not estimates.

**A methodology note**: software pricing changes over time. The figures below reflect
what was published or reported at the time of writing. Confirm current pricing directly
with each vendor before making a purchasing decision.

## Dedicated change management software

**The Change Compass**: starts at US$795 per user, per month for SMB plans, according to
pricing published directly on the company's site, scaling to custom enterprise pricing
with full analytics and advisory support included.

**Prosci certification**: Prosci's own site lists its Change Management Certification
Program at $4,500 USD, covering tuition, course materials, and one year of access to
digital tools and resources. This is a certification program with tool access bundled in,
not standalone software, and digital tool access requires an annual membership renewal
(reported in the roughly $100-200/year range) to remain active after the first year.

**AlignHQ**: publicly listed on G2 at $49/month for a Starter tier and $99/month for a
Professional tier, with a separate Enterprise tier for consultancies needing multi-client
white-labeling.

**ChangeView**: Sole Proprietor at $39/month, Business at $99/month or $990/year,
Enterprise available on request.

## Digital adoption platforms (a different category, often compared side by side anyway)

**Whatfix**: third-party contract data reported by Userpilot (sourced from Vendr) puts
the median annual contract at $31,950/year, with a typical range of roughly
$26,210 to $37,914/year.

**WalkMe**: contract-benchmark figures vary by source. Guidde reports a median of
$79,000/year (range roughly $9,000 to $150,000+, depending on scale), while Tandem
reports a lower median of $43,680/year. The spread itself is telling, WalkMe's pricing
appears to vary significantly by deal size and negotiation.

## What this actually tells you

The dedicated OCM software category splits sharply by audience. Enterprise-focused
platforms (Change Compass) and certification-bundled toolkits (Prosci) price for large
organizations and formal training budgets. Practitioner-focused tools (AlignHQ,
ChangeView) price per-seat at a level that makes sense for an individual consultant or a
small team, without needing a procurement process to justify it.

Digital adoption platforms are a different spend entirely, and usually a much larger one,
reflecting their typical buyer, an enterprise software rollout team, not an individual
change practitioner.

If you're evaluating tools and a vendor won't publish a number anywhere, that's not
necessarily a red flag on its own, but it's worth asking directly, and comparing what you
hear against the real, public numbers above, before you're several sales calls deep.
